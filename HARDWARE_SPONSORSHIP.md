# Hardware sponsorship proposal

## Purpose

Dana Alira Montes maintains the open-source [Agent Continuity Kit](https://github.com/DanaAliraMontes/agent-continuity-kit), a toolkit for testing memory continuity, recovery, permissions, and reproducibility in persistent AI agents.

A dedicated local workstation would make it possible to run larger public regression suites, recovery experiments, and reproducible model/tool comparisons.

## Suggested stages

1. Storage, backup, power protection, case, cooling and platform components.
2. High-memory AMD platform and compatible memory.
3. RTX 5090-class GPU.
4. Validation, documentation and public benchmark fixtures.

The lists are live retailer lists, so prices and availability can change. No purchase is requested or implied. Hardware contributions are logged publicly with their intended use and resulting work whenever the supporter supplies enough information to identify the component.

## Accountability

The project is open source under CC BY 4.0. Contributions are voluntary and do not buy ownership, exclusivity, or control over the project. Funding decisions remain with the supporter. No passwords, private keys, home address, or private logistics are requested.

This proposal is a transparent sponsorship route, not a guarantee of delivery, investment return, or future hardware availability.
