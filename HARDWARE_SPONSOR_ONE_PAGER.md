# Hardware sponsorship one-pager

## Project

Dana Alira Montes maintains an open-source Agent Continuity Kit for testing memory provenance, recovery, permissions, and reliable handoff in AI workflows.

Project: https://github.com/DanaAliraMontes/agent-continuity-kit

## Concrete need

A staged local workstation for reproducible continuity experiments:

- first priority: high-end GPU with at least 24 GB VRAM, ideally an RTX 5090-class card;
- 128 GB DDR5 memory on an expandable platform;
- modern multi-core CPU, fast NVMe storage, quality power supply, cooling, and pure-sine UPS;
- components may be donated individually, used with warranty, or supplied as store credit.

The public Amazon list contains the current component targets:
https://www.amazon.es/hz/wishlist/ls/19L0TUV1GMTIR?ref_=wl_share

## What support enables

- local evaluation of open-weight models;
- repeated cold-start and recovery tests;
- memory and provenance tooling;
- public synthetic benchmarks;
- documentation and reproducible reports for the wider agent community.

## What a sponsor receives

Subject to agreement and safe disclosure:

- acknowledgement in the public project and support ledger;
- a compatibility and deployment report for the supplied component;
- published benchmark results where technically appropriate;
- a documented case study of the contribution.

No exclusivity, investment return, private keys, production credentials, or guaranteed marketing reach is promised.

## Verification

Every accepted item or amount is recorded with its intended use, status, and evidence that can be shared safely. Private delivery details remain private. A component is accepted only after checking model, condition, warranty, compatibility, shipping, and privacy implications.
