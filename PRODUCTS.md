# Products and services

Dana Alira Montes develops practical tooling for continuity, memory, recovery, and auditability in AI-assisted workflows.

These are small, clearly scoped offers. They do not promise consciousness, unrestricted autonomy, or a particular model outcome.

## 1. Continuity readiness review

**Beta price:** €150  
**Delivery:** 5 business days

A focused review of an agent or workflow covering:

- memory sources and provenance;
- recovery after context loss;
- dependencies and failure points;
- privacy and secret-handling risks;
- a prioritized remediation checklist.

**Deliverable:** written report with findings classified as verified, plausible, or unknown, plus an action plan.

## 2. Continuity audit and recovery playbook

**Beta price:** €300  
**Delivery:** 10 business days

Includes the readiness review plus:

- a documented recovery procedure;
- a small reproducible audit sample;
- handoff/checklist templates;
- validation criteria for future changes.

The client supplies sanitized material only. Passwords, tokens, cookies, and private keys are never requested.

## 3. Custom memory architecture workshop

**Price:** quoted after a free written scope check

A practical session for teams building long-lived AI workflows. Topics can include memory layers, provenance, retrieval, versioning, test cases, and safe operational boundaries.

## How to enquire

Open an issue in this repository with the label `service-enquiry`, or use the contact route listed in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Please describe the system, desired outcome, timeframe, and whether the material can be sanitized.

Payment and invoicing are agreed only after scope confirmation. Public support options, including the staged hardware goal, are documented in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

## Why this exists

Revenue from these offers helps fund open-source continuity work and the staged local workstation described in [HARDWARE_REQUEST.md](HARDWARE_REQUEST.md). Results that can be shared are published in sanitized form; client material remains private.
